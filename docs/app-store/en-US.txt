DESCRIPTION
Let your agents work. Meow brings their updates to your iPhone.

Get notified when an AI agent finishes a turn, encounters an error, or needs your attention. Read the context on your phone, then continue in the original tool on your computer.

CONNECT YOUR WORKFLOW
Connect Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor with the Meow command-line tool. Scripts, build jobs, and other services can send notifications through the CLI or HTTP API too. The CLI is available for macOS and Linux.

AN INBOX THAT STAYS ORGANIZED
Browse messages by session, project, or agent. Search downloaded history on your device, including while offline. Mark messages or groups read, or delete the items you no longer need.

READ THE WHOLE RESULT
Open a notification to read the full message, with Markdown, code blocks, and tables. Session and project context help you recognize which task needs you.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose the Meow sound or the default system notification sound. Important messages can use Time Sensitive notifications when allowed by your iOS notification and Focus settings.

CHOOSE YOUR SERVER
Start with the suggested official server, or connect to a Meow server you host yourself. No personal Meow account is required. Messages sent to a server are cached there; downloaded history is stored on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for data handling and retention.

Meow delivers notifications. Replies and decisions stay in the original tool on your computer. Notification delivery requires a working network connection and the relevant system permissions.
